Cache Hardware Explorer

A direct-mapped cache, reads only. Type a (RAM) address, then watch how its bits split up and which hardware they travel through. The cache holds copies of RAM data; its rows are picked by an index, not an address.

    Wires with a slash and a number carry that many bits. Purple = address bits, black = what is stored in the cache, blue = data. In the hit logic, green = 1 and red = 0. The glow around a wire has the color of the step that used it. Grey bits in a slot with valid = 0 are leftovers, not a copy of anything in RAM. The selected slot's bits come out on the wires below it, the same way a row is read out of RAM. Multi-byte words are read little-endian, like RISC-V.
